# Gunplay 1.2.0

Gunplay now includes unloading and ammo preservation from Apocaunloader.

- Hold Reload to unload the current gun; a short press reloads on release.
- Rounds return to the backpack, with overflow dropped as a save-compatible ammo item.
- Changing/removing backpacks preserves excess ammo. Failed drops retain the rounds and retry.
- Identical guns keep separate magazines and hammer/chamber state across switching and saving/loading.
- Apocaunloader 1.3.0 magazine saves carry over unchanged; its settings migrate into Gunplay's new Ammo section.
- Native weapon switching and Apocapocket's extra slots are supported.
- Gunplay takes ownership if standalone Apocaunloader is still installed, preventing duplicate ammo processing.

Apocaunloader is deprecated: update Gunplay and disable the standalone mod. Gunplay HUD remains a separate optional mod.
The existing EnableGunplay switch controls projectile simulation. Ammo settings have separate unloading/overflow switches;
per-gun magazines stay tracked while Gunplay is installed to protect loaded rounds.
