# Gunplay 1.3.0

- Switching to lower-capacity body armor drops one armor plate for every full 50 points of excess armor. Switching from 250 armor to a 100-capacity vest drops three plates.
- Excess below 50 creates no plate and is clamped normally. Failed spawns retain armor and retry, preventing loss or duplicate plates.
- Returned plates are collectible world items registered with the game's item-saving system.
- The Ammo section now appears above Debug. Drop armor overflow is enabled by default and appears immediately after Drop backpack overflow.

Unloading, backpack overflow protection, and independent per-gun magazines remain included. Standalone Apocaunloader remains deprecated.

Validation: 48 logic checks, including 50,601 fractional armor/capacity cases; 42 checks inside the shipped Unity/PlayMaker/Easy Save runtime, repeated successfully with standalone Apocaunloader installed. Native checks cover armor drops, failed-spawn recovery, collectible plate setup, settings order, saves, and duplicate-hook suppression. Mono C# 7 compatibility build also passes.
